## Mathong — Mahjong Solitaire

A Material 3 Mahjong Solitaire game for Android.

### Highlights
- 14 hand-built layouts from the quick 32-tile **Pocket** to the classic 144-tile **Turtle** and the towering **Ziggurat**
- Every deal is guaranteed solvable, and shuffles keep the board solvable
- Smart hints that look ahead to avoid dead ends, unlimited undo / redo, shuffle
- Daily Challenge with streaks and a completion calendar
- Combo scoring, time bonus and 3-star ratings
- 18 achievements and detailed per-layout statistics
- Material You dynamic color, light / dark themes, 6 tile styles, 5 board backgrounds
- Hand-drawn tile artwork, synthesized sound effects, haptics, pinch-to-zoom
- Auto-save: pick up any game exactly where you left off

Install `Mathong-<tag>.apk` (release build). The `-debug` APK can be installed side by side.
